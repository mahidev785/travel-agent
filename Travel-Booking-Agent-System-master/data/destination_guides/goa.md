# Goa destination guide

- North Goa (Baga, Anjuna) is nightlife and beach shacks; South Goa (Palolem, Agonda) is quieter.
- Dudhsagar Falls is a half-day trip, best in monsoon or just after.
- Old Goa's churches (Basilica of Bom Jesus) are a UNESCO World Heritage site.
- Flea markets at Anjuna (Wednesday) and Arpora (Saturday night) are worth an evening.
- Vegetarian Goan food centers on coconut curries and Konkani thalis.
- Water sports (parasailing, jet-ski) cluster around Baga and Calangute beaches.

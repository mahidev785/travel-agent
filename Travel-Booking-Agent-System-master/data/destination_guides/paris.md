# Paris destination guide

- The Louvre and Musee d'Orsay cover most major art movements; go early to beat queues.
- Montmartre's cobbled streets and Sacre-Coeur are best explored on foot in the morning.
- The Seine river cruise at sunset is touristy but genuinely worth it for the views.
- Le Marais has the best mix of small cafes, vintage shops, and falafel stands.
- Vegetarian travelers do well at Le Potager du Marais and Season Marais.
- Versailles is a full-day trip; book timed tickets in advance.

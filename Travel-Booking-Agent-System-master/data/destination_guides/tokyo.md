# Tokyo destination guide

- Senso-ji temple in Asakusa is the classic first stop, especially at sunrise.
- Shibuya crossing and the Shibuya Sky observation deck pair well in one afternoon.
- TeamLab Planets is an immersive digital art museum; book tickets weeks ahead.
- Tsukiji Outer Market is best for breakfast sushi and street food.
- Vegetarian and vegan options are concentrated around Shimokitazawa.
- A day trip to Hakone gives you Mt. Fuji views and hot springs (onsen).

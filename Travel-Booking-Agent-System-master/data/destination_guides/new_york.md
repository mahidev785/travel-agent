# New York destination guide

- Central Park, the Met, and the Upper East Side pair well for a museum-and-park day.
- The High Line and Chelsea Market are a good combined afternoon walk.
- Broadway shows sell same-day rush/lottery tickets for a fraction of full price.
- Brooklyn's Dumbo and Williamsburg are worth a half-day for views and food.
- Vegetarian and vegan food is easy to find city-wide, especially in the East Village.
- The 9/11 Memorial and One World Observatory anchor a downtown history day.
